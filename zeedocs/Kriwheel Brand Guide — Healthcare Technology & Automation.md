# 2. What Kriwheel Represents

Kriwheel represents the **connected movement of healthcare operations**.

The brand is built around the idea that healthcare works better when its people, processes, information, and systems work together.

The name should therefore be understood conceptually as:

> **Kriwheel is the technology that keeps the moving parts of healthcare connected and moving forward.**

This is not a claim that "Kri" literally means healthcare.

**Kri** is the distinctive, proprietary element of the name.

**Wheel** provides the conceptual metaphor:

- Movement
- Connection
- Continuity
- Progress
- Interdependence
- Systems working together

Together, they create a brand that represents:

> **Technology that keeps healthcare moving.**

# 3. The Brand Problem We Exist to Solve

- Reception

- Laboratories

Information gets copied manually.

Kriwheel exists to reduce that friction.

### Our role

We turn complicated healthcare workflows into reliable digital systems.

# 13. Customer Problems We Solve

Kriwheel should consistently speak about problems rather than technologies.

### Problem: Fragmented systems

Different parts of the organization operate independently.

**Kriwheel:** Connects systems and workflows.

### Problem: Repetitive administrative work

Staff spend time doing tasks that could be automated.

**Kriwheel:** Automates repetitive workflows.

### Problem: Poor operational visibility

Management cannot easily see what is happening.

**Kriwheel:** Builds dashboards, reporting, and operational systems.

### Problem: Manual patient communication

Staff repeatedly call or message patients.

**Kriwheel:** Automates communication and follow-up.

### Problem: Software that does not fit the organization

Generic tools force organizations to work around the software.

**Kriwheel:** Builds software around the organization's actual workflow.

### Problem: Technology doesn't communicate

Different systems contain information that cannot easily move between them.

**Kriwheel:** Builds integrations and data flows.

Avoid appearing:

- Cold
- Clinical
- Generic
- Trend-chasing
- "AI-first"
- Startup-hype driven
- Cheap
- Like a freelancer
- Like a general web agency

Avoid phrases such as:

> "Revolutionizing healthcare."

> "Transforming the future of medicine."

> "Cutting-edge solutions."

> "Innovative digital solutions."

> "Next-generation healthcare."

> "Connect your patient and billing workflows."

> "Replace repetitive administrative work."

# 21. Brand Story

Healthcare is a system of moving parts.

A patient needs to find the right provider. A clinic needs to manage appointments. A doctor needs access to information. A laboratory needs to return results. A pharmacy needs to fulfill prescriptions. A team needs to communicate. Management needs to understand what is happening.

When those parts are disconnected, people compensate with phone calls, spreadsheets, paper, repetitive data entry, and manual follow-ups.

Kriwheel exists to reduce that friction.

We build software, automation, and integrations that connect the moving parts of healthcare and help organizations operate with greater clarity and efficiency.

The wheel represents movement and connection—not transportation, but the idea that a system works when its parts move together.

**Kriwheel keeps healthcare moving.**

Kriwheel should initially remain a **single master brand**.

### Parent

**Kriwheel**

### Services

**Kriwheel Software**

**Kriwheel Automation**

**Kriwheel Integrations**

These do not necessarily need to be separate brands.

# 27. Brand Principles

Every Kriwheel product or project should follow these principles.

### 1. Solve the workflow, not just the feature.

Understand what the organization is actually trying to accomplish.

### 2. Reduce friction.

Technology should make work easier, not add another system people have to fight.

### 3. Connect the moving parts.

Prefer coherent systems over isolated features.

### 4. Automate deliberately.

Automation should improve reliability and reduce repetitive work.

### 5. Build for reality.

Healthcare software must work under real operational constraints.

### 6. Earn trust through engineering.

Security, reliability, data integrity, and maintainability matter.

### 7. Start with the problem.

Technology is the means, not the product.

# 29. The Kriwheel Test

Before launching a new service, product, piece of content, or partnership, ask:

### Does it relate to healthcare?

If no, question it.

### Does it solve an operational problem?

If no, question it.

### Does software, automation, or integration meaningfully improve the problem?

If no, it may not belong.

### Does it strengthen our expertise?

If no, question it.

### Could this eventually become a reusable capability or product?

If yes, pay particular attention.
